# 🎯 Project Objective

This project automates the complete analytics workflow — from raw data auditing and cleaning to feature engineering, Star Schema modelling, SQL-based KPI generation, and interactive Power BI reporting.

Using **Windows Task Scheduler**, the pipeline automatically executes the Python workflow at scheduled intervals, processes new datasets with the same schema, updates the SQLite database, regenerates KPIs, and prepares data for reporting with minimal manual intervention.

The pipeline is designed to handle future datasets with the same structure, ensuring a scalable and reusable analytics workflow.

# 📊 Power BI Dashboard

The dashboard is designed as a **three-page analytical story**, guiding users from a high-level market overview to detailed model-level insights.

It enables business users to explore key performance metrics, identify market trends, and answer important business questions without manually analysing raw data.

---

## 📈 Market Performance

The landing page answers:

> **"How is the business performing?"**

It provides an executive overview through:

- Sales and revenue trends
- Year-over-Year growth analysis
- Manufacturer market share
- City-wise EV adoption
- Segment performance
- Individual vs Fleet buyer comparison

---

## ⚡ Utilisation Analytics

This page shifts the focus from sales to:

> **"How are customers using EVs and how are vehicles performing?"**

It analyses:

- Urban vs Rural usage behaviour
- Manufacturer-wise range comparison
- Charging time analysis
- Battery efficiency performance
- Charging type distribution

---

## 🚘 Model Drill-Through

The final page provides detailed model-level analysis.

Users can identify:

- Best-selling models
- Fastest-growing models
- Highest revenue-generating models
- Model-level sales comparison
- Customer preferences
- Charging behaviour by model

# 💡 Key Insights

- **Tata Motors** leads the market with a **40.78% market share**, followed by **Mahindra Electric (25.86%)**.

- **Mumbai and Pune** are the strongest EV adoption markets, contributing significantly to overall sales.

- **Individual buyers contribute nearly 70% of total sales**, indicating strong growth potential in the commercial and fleet segment.

- **Rural markets show higher fast-charging adoption** despite lower average vehicle range, highlighting increasing EV acceptance beyond urban areas.

- **Budget and Mid-Range vehicles generate the highest sales volume**, while Premium vehicles contribute higher revenue per unit.

- **Premium vehicles have the longest average charging time (9 hours)** despite offering superior driving range.

- **Year-over-Year market growth stands at 50.52%**, indicating strong EV market expansion.

---

# ✅ Business Recommendations

### Increase Commercial Sales

Individual buyers contribute around **70%** of total sales, while commercial adoption remains lower. Expanding partnerships with fleet operators, logistics companies, and ride-sharing services can unlock additional growth opportunities.

### Improve Charging Accessibility in Rural Areas

Rural customers already show strong fast-charging adoption. Improving charging accessibility can support further EV adoption in these markets.

### Focus on Budget and Mid-Range Models

These segments generate the highest sales volume. Continued investment and targeted marketing can maximize growth while positioning Premium models for higher-value customers.

### Strengthen Presence in Emerging Cities

While Mumbai and Pune lead the market, cities such as Wardha and Yavatmal present opportunities for expansion through localized marketing and dealership growth.

### Improve Premium Vehicle Charging Speed

Premium models provide excellent range but require longer charging times. Faster charging capability can improve customer experience and competitiveness.

### Promote Battery Efficiency as a Competitive Advantage

Manufacturers with higher efficiency should highlight lower running costs to attract customers and strengthen market positioning.
